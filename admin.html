Understanding the customer signup flow

Customer Signup Flow
1
Customer signs up on your website with their phone & email. They get an OTP code by email.
2
Their account is saved in the Pending List in your MongoDB database — not yet active.
3
You come here and enter their phone number → click Verify. Their account becomes active.
4
Customer can now log in, browse your catalog, and place orders on your website.
